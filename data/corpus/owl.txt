The Web Ontology Language (OWL) is a family of ontology languages developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) for authoring ontologies. OWL is built on top of RDF and is used to describe and add semantics to RDF data. Alongside RDF Schema (RDFS) and SHACL, OWL is one of the ontology languages used to describe the classes, properties, and relationships in RDF data. OWL enables richer expression of logical relationships than RDF Schema alone.
