The Resource Description Framework (RDF) is a standard developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) for describing and exchanging graph data. RDF represents information as a directed, labeled graph of statements. Each statement is a triple of the form subject-predicate-object. RDF was adopted as a W3C recommendation in 1999, with RDF 1.0 published in 2004 and RDF 1.1 in 2014. RDF is an abstract data model with several serialization formats, including Turtle, TriG, N-Triples, JSON-LD, and RDF/XML. RDF triples are commonly stored in a type of database called a triplestore.
